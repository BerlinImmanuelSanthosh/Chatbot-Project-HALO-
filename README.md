## 📍 Overview

<code>❯ Halo is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to help students organize their studies intelligently. Instead of juggling scattered notes, random timetables, and last-minute panic, Halo centralizes learning by generating structured notes, personalized study schedules, and topic-wise guidance from minimal user input.

The system allows users to upload their subjects and topics. Based on this input, Halo automatically creates clear, concise study notes and a non-conflicting study plan tailored to the user’s academic load. If a user doesn’t provide topics, Halo adapts by asking for the user’s college and year of study, then generates relevant content accordingly.

Halo focuses on practicality. It prioritizes clarity, time management, and personalization over generic AI responses. The goal is simple: reduce cognitive overload and help students study smarter, not longer.

Key Highlights

AI-generated notes from user-provided subjects and topics

Personalized study schedules with no overlapping or unrealistic timelines

Adaptive flow for users without predefined topics

Designed specifically for students and academic use cases

Clean, user-centric approach to learning assistance

Halo acts less like a chatbot and more like a quiet study partner that actually understands deadlines, priorities, and human limits.</code>

## 👾 Features

<code>❯ AI-Generated Study Notes
Converts subjects and topics into structured, easy-to-understand notes tailored for academic learning.

Personalized Study Schedule
Automatically creates a realistic, non-conflicting study plan based on the user’s subjects, workload, and available time.

Adaptive Input Handling
If topics aren’t provided, Halo intelligently asks for the user’s college and year of study to generate relevant content.

Topic-Wise Organization
Notes and schedules are organized by subject and topic, making revision faster and less chaotic.

Student-Centric Design
Built specifically for students, focusing on clarity, time management, and reducing study overload.

Scalable Learning Assistant
Designed to handle multiple subjects and semesters without breaking into nonsense responses.

Minimal User Effort
Requires only basic input to generate meaningful outputs, avoiding unnecessary setup or complexity.</code>
